Weight loss that adapts to your body, and stays on your phone.

For iPhone and Apple Watch.

  • Adaptive daily budget, recalculated every Sunday from your real weight trend.
  • Readiness score from heart-rate variability, sleep and cycle data, which eases your target on harder days.
  • Protein floor based on your lean mass.
  • Nutrition guidance from your lab results, such as HbA1c, cholesterol and vitamin D.
  • Fast logging by search, barcode, voice or photo, on iPhone or Apple Watch.
  • Private by design: no account, no servers, no tracking.

MetaboSync offers nutrition and lifestyle guidance. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
