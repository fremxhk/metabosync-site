Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

In short: MetaboSync has no servers. Your health data, meals, lab results and settings stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We operate no developer backend and include no analytics, advertising or tracking code. An optional barcode lookup, off by default, contacts Open Food Facts as explained below.

Who we are

MetaboSync is published by Ho Kit Cheung ("we"). This policy covers the MetaboSync app for iPhone and Apple Watch, including its widgets, Siri shortcuts and downloadable food databases.

What data the app uses, and where it stays

DataWhere it lives
Health data you allow it to read (heart-rate variability, sleep, workouts, energy, steps, weight and body composition, blood glucose, cycle data, and lab results from Health Records)Apple Health on your device. MetaboSync reads it only with your permission, to compute your readiness score, calorie budget and nutrition guidance on the device.
Meals and weigh-ins you logWritten to Apple Health, where you can view or delete them at any time.
Calculation history, lab results you enter or scan, and preferencesA local database inside the app on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection.
Photos, lab documents and voice inputProcessed on the device in memory. Lab documents are deleted after reading unless you choose to keep them. Audio is never saved.

What we never do

  • We operate no server, account system or cloud database, so we never receive your data.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software is included.
  • Health data is never used for advertising and is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Health data is not stored in iCloud Drive or CloudKit by the app.
  • AI features use Apple Intelligence's on-device model only. Nothing is sent to a cloud AI service.

Optional barcode lookup

Food search and bundled barcode matching work offline. If you explicitly enable online barcode lookup in Settings, an unrecognized barcode is sent to the independent Open Food Facts service. That provider receives the barcode, your IP address and an app-level User-Agent as part of the request. No Health values, meal photos, speech, free-text searches, account identifiers or AI prompts are sent. The request uses no cookies or app account. Product results are cached locally for 30 days; you can clear the cache or disable lookup in Settings. Provider handling is governed by Open Food Facts privacy information. AI processing remains on device.

Open Food Facts product data is credited to its contributors under ODbL v1.0. Source credits, full licence notices and the separate redistributable public food dataset are available in Settings.

Apple services

Some Apple features work through Apple's own services under Apple's Privacy Policy, not ours:

  • Apple Health may sync your Health data between your devices if you have turned that on in iCloud settings.
  • iCloud Backup includes the app's local database if you use iCloud Backup, so it can be restored to a new phone.
  • In-app purchases are handled by the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or any identifier for you.
  • Food database downloads come from Apple's servers and contain no personal data.
  • Siri requests are handled as described by Apple. The app's shortcuts do not include health values in the text they send.
  • App Store analytics and crash reports are shared with us only if you opt in on your device, and only in aggregated, anonymous form.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw Health access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.
  • Export your data as a passphrase-encrypted file from the app's Settings.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app. Meals and weigh-ins it wrote remain in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Children

MetaboSync is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly process data from children.

Not medical advice

MetaboSync offers nutrition and lifestyle guidance based on patterns in your data. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk with a clinician about your results.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: see the Support page.
