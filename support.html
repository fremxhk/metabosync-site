Contact us: open a support request (free GitHub account needed). Please don't include health data or lab results in your message. We reply within a few working days.

Frequently asked questions

The app shows no data or a "safe minimum" message.

MetaboSync needs a few days of Apple Health data. Check Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › MetaboSync and allow the data types listed, then open the app again. Weigh in regularly (a smart scale that writes to Apple Health works best).

How is my calorie budget calculated?

Every Sunday the app estimates your energy expenditure from your weight trend and logged intake, then sets a daily budget for your chosen weekly deficit. On low-readiness days it eases the target. The budget never goes below a safe minimum.

Does my data leave my phone?

No. MetaboSync has no servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Photo or voice logging isn't available.

These use Apple Intelligence on your device, which needs a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Search and barcode logging work on every device.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Use Apple's Quick Start transfer or restore from an iCloud backup. You can also export an encrypted file from Settings in the app and import it on the new phone.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Tap "Restore purchases" on the MetaboSync Pro screen. To cancel, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Is this medical advice?

No. MetaboSync offers nutrition and lifestyle guidance based on patterns in your data. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk with a clinician about your results.
